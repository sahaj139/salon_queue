# Salon Queue — Brief

## Problem
On weekends customers sit 1–2 hours in the salon without knowing their turn.
Some leave; others argue about who came first. The receptionist has no clear
view of who is next for which barber.

## Users
- **Customer** — joins from their phone (or is added as a walk-in), watches their turn.
- **Receptionist / owner** — runs the queue on a tablet: walk-ins, call next, no-shows.
- **Barber** — sees their current and next customer on a phone, taps Start / Done.
- **Owner** — sets services, prices, barbers on duty, opening hours, open/close online queue.
- **Waiting area TV** — shows who is in which chair and who is next.

## Must-have features
1. Join the queue from your phone: name, phone, 1–3 services, a barber or "Any barber".
2. Live status: token, position, estimated wait, "Start coming" and "You're next!" alerts,
   full-screen "It's your turn!" when called.
3. Reception dashboard: add walk-ins, Call next per barber, No-show, Cancel, Re-add.
4. Barber screen: current customer, Start / Done, next customer, On break / Available.
5. Waiting-area TV: who is with which barber + next 5 tokens, flash + chime on call.
6. Owner settings: services (EN/GU name, minutes, price, on/off), barbers on duty,
   open/close online queue, hours, today's stats.
7. English / Gujarati toggle on every screen.
8. Phone, tablet and TV stay in sync within 5 seconds.

## Don't build
Online payment, future-date appointments, SMS/WhatsApp, loyalty points,
multiple branches, customer accounts/passwords, reviews.

## Look & feel
Premium salon: dark charcoal (#1F2937) + gold (#D4A017), rounded cards, mobile-first.
Labels in English and Gujarati.
